## Description
Prem Oracle is a private weekly score-prediction game you play with your mates
through invitations.

How it works:
- Someone shares an invite. You open it, pick the name your mates will see, and
  you're in — one step, no account to set up.
- Each week the league host chooses that week's fixtures.
- Everyone predicts the final scores. Predictions lock at kick-off, so nobody
  can see anyone else's picks until the whistle.
- When the results are in, the week settles and the league table updates —
  weekly standings and a running season table, with places and movement.

It's built for small groups of friends: no public leaderboards, no noise — just
your league, your mates, and bragging rights each week.

Prediction, reminders and recovery are yours to control: choose whether to get a
kick-off reminder, and keep a private recovery code so you can restore your
account on another device.
